About our school

Akurana Muslim Balika Maha Vidyalaya is a government girls’ school for Grades 1 to 13, with around 1,500 to 1,650 students and over 56 teachers. It belongs to the Katugastota Education Zone, Akurana Educational Division. The school has a steady record in the Grade 5 Scholarship and G.C.E. Ordinary Level examinations.

PrincipalSuheera Siddique

Our story

From 101 students in 1946 to well over 1,500 today.

  • 1946Founded by village headman Seyyad Mohamed as a mixed school with 101 students.
  • 1993Authorized to teach Grade 11.
  • 1996Advanced Level (A/L) classes introduced.
  • 2003English medium introduced from Grade 6.
